Hi! I'm Ruixi (睿熙) Luo (罗), welcome to my homepage!

I received my B.E. in Intelligence Science and Technology from Sun Yat-sen University in 2023 (GPA rank: 7/135). I am currently completing my M.S. in Control Science and Engineering at [School of Intelligent Systems Engineering of Sun Yat-sen University](https://ise.sysu.edu.cn/), where I work with Professor [Kai Jin](https://cscjjk.github.io) on theoretical computer science, algorithm design, and computational geometry. Starting from August 2026, I will be a Ph.D. student in [Decisions, Operations and Technology at the Faculty of Business Administration](https://www.bschool.cuhk.edu.hk/departments/decisions-operations-and-technology/), The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Professor [Wei Tang](https://wtang.org/).

Outside of academia, I am passionate about history and enjoy exploring it through visualizations — especially historical maps and family genealogies. I am also a fan of Paradox Interactive games, from mapping dynastic intrigues in Crusader Kings to building galactic empires in Stellaris.
