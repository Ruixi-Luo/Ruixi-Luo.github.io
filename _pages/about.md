Hi! I’m Ruixi(睿熙) Luo(罗), welcome to my homepage!

I received my B.E. in Intelligence Science and Technology from [School of Intelligent Systems Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University](https://ise.sysu.edu.cn/) in 2023 (GPA rank: 7/135). 

I received my M.E. in Control Science and Engineering at [School of Intelligent Systems Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University](https://ise.sysu.edu.cn/), where I worked with Professor [Kai Jin](https://cscjjk.github.io) on theoretical computer science, algorithm design, and computational geometry. 

Starting from August 2026, I will be a Ph.D. student in [Decisions, Operations and Technology at the Faculty of Business Administration, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.bschool.cuhk.edu.hk/departments/decisions-operations-and-technology/), supervised by Professor [Wei Tang](https://wtang.org/).
